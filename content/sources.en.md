This site prefers **primary sources**: the text of the law itself, and official pages from
Parliament, the courts, Elections Canada, the Governor General, federal departments, and
provincial and territorial legislatures and governments. A reputable secondary source,
such as the Canadian Encyclopedia, is used only when no official page covers the point.

Each page lists its own sources at the bottom, often with the exact sections it relies on.
This page gathers them all in one place. Links marked as unverified couldn't be checked
automatically and need a human to confirm them in a browser.

## The Constitution and legislation

### The Constitution Acts

- [The Constitution Acts, 1867 to 1982](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/): table of contents for the official consolidated text (Justice Laws)
- [Constitution Act, 1867](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-1.html): preamble and Parts I–IV (ss. 1–52), including executive power, Parliament and the Senate
- [Constitution Act, 1867, ss. 53–90](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-2.html): money bills, royal assent, Lieutenant Governors and provincial constitutions
- [Constitution Act, 1867, Parts VI–VIII](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-3.html): the division of powers (ss. 91–95), the courts (ss. 96–101) and public revenue
- [Constitution Act, 1867, ss. 128 and 146](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-4.html): the oath of allegiance and the admission of other colonies
- [Constitution Act, 1982, Parts I–II](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-12.html): the Charter (ss. 1–34) and Aboriginal and treaty rights (s. 35)
- [Constitution Act, 1982, Parts III–VII](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/page-13.html): equalization (s. 36), the amending formula (ss. 38–49) and constitutional supremacy (s. 52)
- [Endnotes to the Constitution Acts](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/const/endNotes.html): the history of individual amendments
