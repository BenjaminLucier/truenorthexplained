## Why this exists

Most people living in Canada, whether they were born here or just arrived, couldn't
explain how the country is actually governed. Who picks the Prime Minister? What does
the King do? Why do provinces run hospitals but Ottawa runs criminal law? Where do the
courts fit?

This site started as one person's attempt to learn exactly that, written down as they
went. The goal is a place where anyone can get an accurate picture in ten minutes, and
go as deep as they want after that.

## How it's written

- **Plain language.** No background assumed. Every term is defined in the
  [glossary](/glossary).
- **Two depths.** Each topic has a short main page, plus optional deep dives.
- **Neutral.** The site explains how things work, not whether they should. Where
  Canadians genuinely disagree, it says so and summarizes the main views.
- **Sourced.** Claims link to primary sources wherever possible: the Constitution and
  other laws, Parliament, the courts, and official government sites. See
  [Sources](/sources).

## Quizzes and your data

Quiz progress is saved only in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent
anywhere, and there are no accounts or tracking.

## Languages

Canada has two official languages, so this site is being built to work in both English
and French.

## Not legal advice

This is an educational overview. It isn't legal advice, and it simplifies on purpose.
For anything that affects you personally, check the official source or talk to a
lawyer.
