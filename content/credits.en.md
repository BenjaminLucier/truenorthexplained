This site is built from openly licensed material. Every dataset is downloaded by a
script that can be re-run, and records where it came from, its licence and when it was
fetched. This page lists all of it.

## Datasets

The charts, timelines and tables on the reference pages ([Prime ministers](/prime-ministers),
[Monarchs and governors general](/governors-general) and [Demographics](/demographics)),
and the [treaty map](/learn/indigenous/treaties), are built from these datasets when the
site is built.

<tn-widget name="credits" data-part="datasets"></tn-widget>

- **Wikidata** is a free knowledge base run by the Wikimedia Foundation. Its data is in
  the public domain under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  The script checks who currently holds each office against the matching Wikipedia
  article, because Wikidata can lag behind an election, and records any difference.
- **Statistics Canada** data is used under the
  [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
  This site is not endorsed by Statistics Canada.
- **Finance Canada** (the Fiscal Reference Tables and the Historical Transfer Tables)
  publishes its data under the same
  [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
  The **Bank of Canada**'s interest rates come from its Valet data service, used under the
  Bank's [terms of use](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/terms/), which allow free use with
  attribution. They feed the charts on [federal spending](/learn/government/the-budget),
  [transfers and equalization](/learn/federalism/money-and-equalization) and
  [the Bank of Canada](/learn/government/bank-of-canada-vs-mint). Neither endorses this site.
- **Treaty areas** come from the Historic Treaties and Modern Treaties datasets of
  Crown–Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs Canada, used under the
  [Open Government Licence – Canada](https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada).
  The boundaries are simplified for the map and are not legal boundaries.

## Flags

The flags of Canada and the provinces and territories come from Wikimedia Commons. Most
are marked as public domain there; any that need attribution are credited below. Each
file's licence and author are listed as recorded on Commons.

## Wikipedia

The site's writers use a snapshot of about 150 English Wikipedia articles as background
reading when checking and updating pages. Wikipedia text is available under the
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Where a page adapts Wikipedia material, it links to the article in its sources.

## Other sources

Each page lists the official sources it relies on, such as legislation on Justice Laws,
Parliament, Elections Canada and the courts. They're gathered on the [Sources](/sources)
page.
