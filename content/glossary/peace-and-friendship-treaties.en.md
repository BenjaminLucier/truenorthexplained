---
term: Peace and Friendship Treaties
see:
  - /learn/indigenous/treaties
  - /provinces/nova-scotia
---

Historic treaties of the 1700s with the Mi'kmaq, Wolastoqiyik and Peskotomuhkati in the Maritimes and Gaspé. They did not surrender land; *R. v. Marshall* (1999) held they protect a Mi'kmaq right to fish and trade for a moderate livelihood.
