The main trial court of general jurisdiction in each province and territory, also called a **section 96 court** because its judges are appointed and paid by the federal government. It hears the most serious criminal cases, major civil suits and divorce. Its name varies: Court of King's Bench, Superior Court or, confusingly, Supreme Court.
