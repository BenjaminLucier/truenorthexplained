---
term: Notwithstanding clause
see:
  - /learn/charter/notwithstanding-clause
  - /learn/charter
---

Section 33 of the Charter, which lets Parliament or a provincial legislature declare that a law operates despite the fundamental freedoms (s. 2), legal rights (ss. 7–14) or equality rights (s. 15). Each use expires after five years unless re-enacted.
