---
term: Patriation Reference
see:
  - /learn/constitution/patriation-1982
  - /learn/constitution/conventions
---

The 1981 Supreme Court opinion that Ottawa could legally ask Britain to amend the Constitution without the provinces, but that a convention required, in the judgment's words, "at least a substantial measure of provincial consent" (some summaries say "substantial degree"). It also set the test for identifying conventions.
