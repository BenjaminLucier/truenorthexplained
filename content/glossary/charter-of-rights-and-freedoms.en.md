---
term: Charter of Rights and Freedoms
see:
  - /learn/charter
  - /learn/charter/your-rights
---

Part I of the Constitution Act, 1982 (ss. 1–34), which protects rights such as freedom of expression, the right to vote, legal rights and equality against **governments**. Rights can be limited under s. 1 and some can be overridden under s. 33.
