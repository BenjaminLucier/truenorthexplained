---
term: City charter
see:
  - /provinces/alberta
  - /provinces/british-columbia
  - /learn/federalism/municipalities
---

A province's special law or set of rules for one large city, giving it different or broader powers than other municipalities. Examples include the Vancouver Charter, The City of Winnipeg Charter, the Halifax Regional Municipality Charter, the City of Toronto Act, 2006 and the charters for Calgary and Edmonton.
