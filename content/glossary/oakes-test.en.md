---
term: Oakes test
see:
  - /learn/charter/reasonable-limits
  - /learn/charter/landmark-cases
---

The test from *R. v. Oakes* (1986) for justifying a limit on a Charter right under s. 1: a pressing and substantial objective, a rational connection, minimal impairment of the right, and benefits that outweigh the harms. The government must pass every step.
