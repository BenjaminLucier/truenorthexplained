# How does Canada actually work?

A plain-language guide to Canada's government, laws and Constitution, for anyone
curious. You can get the gist in ten minutes, and go deeper whenever you like.

[Take the 10-minute tour](/tour) · [Start with the big picture](/learn/big-picture)

## The one-sentence answer

Canada is a **federal parliamentary democracy** and a **constitutional monarchy**.

- **Constitutional monarchy:** the King is head of state, but elected politicians
  govern in his name.
- **Parliamentary democracy:** you elect a local MP, and the government is whoever can
  keep the House of Commons' support.
- **Federal:** power is split between Ottawa and the provinces, each with its own areas.
